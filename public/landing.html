Questions people ask first

How accurate is it?

Totals are recalculated from the line items. If the numbers don't add up, or a date could be read two ways, the invoice is flagged so you can confirm before exporting.

Are my invoices safe?

Files are used only to produce your report and are deleted afterward. We never use your documents for anything else.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, JPG and PNG photos or scans, and Excel or CSV files.

Can I get the data into my accounting software?

You can export to Excel or CSV today. Direct connections to accounting software are on the roadmap.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can cancel from your account page.
